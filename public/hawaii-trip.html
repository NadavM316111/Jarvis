Pro Tips for the Family

Book Early

Reserve Haleakala sunrise permits, Pearl Harbor tickets, and Kualoa Ranch tours at least 2-3 months in advance. They sell out fast!

Pack Smart

Bring reef-safe sunscreen (required by law!), water shoes for rocky beaches, and layers for the volcano. Leave room for souvenirs!

Timing is Everything

Hit popular spots early morning (before 9 AM) or late afternoon. Midday crowds are intense, plus you'll avoid the hottest sun.

Respect the Aina

Hawaiian word for "land." Don't touch sea turtles, stay on marked trails, and never stack rocks (it's culturally disrespectful).
