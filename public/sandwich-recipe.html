The Ultimate Sandwich

A masterpiece between two slices of bread

Ingredients

* 2 slices artisan bread
* 3 slices turkey or ham
* 2 slices Swiss cheese
* Fresh lettuce leaves
* 2 tomato slices
* 1/4 avocado, sliced
* 2 tbsp mayo
* 1 tbsp Dijon mustard

Instructions

1

Prep the Bread

Lightly toast your artisan bread slices until golden brown. This adds a satisfying crunch and prevents sogginess.

2

Spread the Goodness

Apply mayo generously on one slice and Dijon mustard on the other. This creates the perfect flavor foundation.

3

Layer the Cheese

Place Swiss cheese directly on the bread so it slightly melts from the warmth of the toast.

4

Add the Protein

Fold the turkey or ham slices loosely - don't lay them flat! This creates air pockets for better texture.

5

Fresh Veggies

Add crisp lettuce, ripe tomato slices, and creamy avocado. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.

6

Close and Slice

Press gently, slice diagonally (the fancy way!), and enjoy your masterpiece!

Pro Tips

  • Use room temperature ingredients for best flavor
  • Pat your lettuce dry to prevent sogginess
  • Diagonal cuts make everything taste better
  • Add bacon for extra deliciousness
