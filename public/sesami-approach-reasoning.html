Approach Reasoning

The strategic thinking behind Sesami's design philosophy

Core Design Philosophy

Sesami was built on a fundamental belief: technology should enhance human connection, not replace it. Every design decision was filtered through this lens.

Guiding Principle: "Technology as a bridge, not a barrier, between parent and child."

Why a Teddy Bear Form Factor?

1

Universal Comfort Object

Teddy bears have been children's comfort objects for over 100 years. The form factor immediately signals safety, warmth, and companionship to children.

2

Tactile Engagement

Unlike hard plastic toys or screens, a soft teddy encourages physical interaction - hugging, carrying, sleeping with - which supports emotional development.

3

Gender Neutral Appeal

Teddy bears are universally loved across genders, cultures, and backgrounds, making Sesami accessible to all families.

Why Parent Voice Cloning?

Research shows children respond 3x more attentively to their parent's voice compared to unfamiliar voices. By cloning the parent's voice, Sesami leverages this biological connection to maximize learning engagement while providing emotional comfort.

Why Screen-Free?

The Case Against Screens (Ages 0-5)

  • AAP Guidelines: American Academy of Pediatrics recommends limiting screen time for children under 5
  • Attention Development: Rapid visual stimulation from screens can impact developing attention systems
  • Language Acquisition: Face-to-face (or voice-to-voice) interaction is superior for language development
  • Imagination: Audio-only engagement promotes visualization and creative thinking
Our Choice: By intentionally excluding screens, Sesami forces engagement through listening, speaking, and imagining - the core building blocks of early cognitive development.
