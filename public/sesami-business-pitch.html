2Our Solution

Sesami is an AI-powered educational companion that speaks with the parent's own voice. Built into a comforting teddy bear form factor, it provides active engagement through conversation, storytelling, and developmental activities - all without a screen.

Children respond naturally to their parent's voice, creating immediate trust and engagement. Our voice cloning technology requires just 3 minutes of recordings to generate a warm, natural-sounding clone that 94% of children recognize as their parent.
