Business Plan

Executive Summary

Sesami is positioned to capture a significant share of the $32 billion educational toy market by offering a unique, technology-enhanced companion that addresses parent concerns about screen time while supporting early childhood development.

Revenue Model

  • Hardware: Sesami Bear - $149 (40% margin)
  • Subscription: Sesami Premium - $9.99/month (insights, new activities)
  • Accessories: Activity card packs - $19.99 each
  • Enterprise: Daycare/preschool licensing

Go-to-Market Strategy

Phase 1: Direct-to-consumer launch via website and social media

Phase 2: Retail partnerships (Target, Buy Buy Baby, Amazon)

Phase 3: International expansion and B2B channels

Target: 10,000 units in Year 1, scaling to 100,000 units by Year 3
