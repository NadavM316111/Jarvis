Design Goals, Criteria & Constraints

Engineering Excellence for Early Childhood

Primary Design Goals

SAFE

Child Safety First

All materials non-toxic, no small parts, flame retardant fabrics, meets CPSIA and ASTM F963 standards

ENGAGE

Captivating Interaction

Voice responses under 500ms latency, natural conversation flow, adaptive difficulty levels

DEVELOP

Developmental Impact

Measurable improvements in language, motor skills, attention span, and problem-solving

COMFORT

Emotional Comfort

Parent voice cloning creates familiarity, soft huggable form, soothing interaction patterns

Technical Criteria

Criteria Target Specification Priority
Voice Recognition Accuracy >95% for child speech patterns Critical
Voice Clone Authenticity >90% similarity score (MOS rating 4.2+) Critical
Battery Life 8+ hours active use Critical
Response Latency <500ms end-to-end High
Weight <1.5 lbs (holdable by 2-year-old) High
Washability Machine washable outer shell High
Data Privacy COPPA compliant, AES-256 encryption Critical

Design Constraints

Regulatory Constraints

CPSIA Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act compliance for children's products
COPPA Children's Online Privacy Protection Act for data collection under 13
ASTM F963 Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Toy Safety
FCC Federal Communications Commission certification for wireless devices
