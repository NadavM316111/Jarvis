Expert Advisory Panel

Sesami's development was guided by a diverse panel of experts across multiple disciplines, ensuring our product meets the highest standards for child development, safety, and technological innovation.

Dr. Sarah Mitchell - Child Developmental Psychologist

The parent voice element is transformative. Children's brains are wired to respond to their caregiver's voice from birth. By using voice cloning technology, Sesami creates a bridge of familiarity that allows children to feel safe while exploring new learning activities.

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, PhD - Stanford Early Childhood Development Lab

Key Recommendations Implemented:

  • Activities structured around developmental milestones (CDC guidelines)
  • Calm, encouraging tone patterns to build confidence
  • Pause-and-wait methodology to encourage child response
  • Positive reinforcement without over-stimulation

Prof. James Chen - AI/ML Specialist

The voice cloning technology Sesami uses is cutting-edge but implemented responsibly. We've achieved 94% voice similarity while maintaining strict ethical boundaries - the AI only learns from parent-provided samples and never generates content outside approved scripts.

Prof. James Chen - MIT Media Lab

Technical Contributions:

  • Developed proprietary voice cloning algorithm requiring only 5 minutes of samples
  • Created emotion-adaptive response system
  • Designed privacy-first architecture with on-device processing
  • Built developmental tracking ML models

Maria Rodriguez - Pediatric Safety Consultant

Every component of Sesami was evaluated for safety from a pediatric perspective. The materials are hypoallergenic, the electronics are fully enclosed, and there are no small parts. This exceeds CPSC standards for children ages 0-5.

Maria Rodriguez - Consumer Product Safety Commission (Former)

Safety Features Implemented:

  • CPSIA-compliant materials throughout
  • Volume limiter (max 70dB to protect hearing)
  • Automatic shutoff after extended use
  • Machine-washable outer shell
  • No choking hazard components
