Initial Discovery Phase

Our preliminary research began with understanding the landscape of early childhood development and the role technology plays in modern parenting. We conducted surveys with over 150 parents of children ages 1-5.

Key Findings

  • Children ages 2-4 spend an average of 2.5 hours daily on screens
  • Parent voice recognition develops as early as 7 months in utero
  • Interactive play increases language development by 40% compared to passive media
  • 85% of parents would pay premium for educational toys that reduce screen dependency

Critical Insight: The attachment to a parent's voice is one of the strongest emotional bonds in early childhood, yet no product currently leverages voice cloning for educational purposes.
