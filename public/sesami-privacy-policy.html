Privacy Policy

Protecting your family's data is our highest priority

Last Updated: May 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Sesami, we understand that you're entrusting us with something precious - your child's developmental journey and your family's private moments. We take this responsibility seriously and have built our entire platform with privacy as the foundation, not an afterthought.

COPPA Compliant

Sesami is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), ensuring the highest standards of protection for children under 13.

Information We Collect

We collect only what's necessary to provide Sesami's services:

  • Parent Voice Samples: Used exclusively for voice cloning to create your child's personalized Sesami experience. Never shared or sold.
  • Interaction Data: Anonymous patterns of how your child engages with activities to generate developmental insights.
  • Account Information: Basic contact details for account management and support.
  • Device Information: Technical data to ensure Sesami works properly in your home.

How We Protect Your Data

Your family's data is protected by enterprise-grade security measures:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All voice data and interactions are encrypted both in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • On-Device Processing: Voice recognition happens locally on the Sesami device whenever possible, minimizing cloud exposure.
  • Zero Data Selling: We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties.
  • Regular Audits: Independent security firms audit our systems quarterly to ensure ongoing protection.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your family's data:

  • Access: Request a complete copy of all data we have about your family at any time.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of all your data, including voice samples, within 30 days.
  • Portability: Export your child's developmental reports and insights in standard formats.
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information in your account.
