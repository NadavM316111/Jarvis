Purpose for Innovation

Why Sesami Exists

The Problem We're Solving

Excessive Screen Time

Children ages 0-5 spend an average of 2.5 hours daily on screens, exceeding AAP recommendations by 150%

Parental Time Constraints

Working parents have limited time for interactive developmental play, averaging only 34 minutes of quality engagement daily

Early Development Gaps

30% of children enter kindergarten without age-appropriate language and social-emotional skills

Our Innovation Purpose

"

To create a developmentally supportive companion that replaces passive screen consumption with active, personalized engagement using the most comforting voice a child knows - their parent's.

"

Innovation Pillars

01

Voice Cloning Technology

Advanced AI that authentically replicates parent voices for emotional connection

02

Developmental Science

Activities designed by child psychologists targeting key growth milestones

03

Parent Insights

Real-time tracking and recommendations for supporting child development

04

Familiar Form Factor

Teddy bear design creates comfort and encourages natural interaction
