How RFID Technology Works

RFID in Sesami

Sesami utilizes Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) technology to create interactive learning experiences through physical play objects.

How It Works: Small RFID tags embedded in activity cards and toys communicate with Sesami's internal reader, triggering specific voice responses and activities.

Technical Specifications

  • Frequency: 13.56 MHz (NFC-compatible)
  • Read Range: 2-4 inches (safe for close interaction)
  • Tag Type: Passive NTAG215 chips
  • Response Time: Under 100ms
  • Power Consumption: Minimal (passive tags require no battery)

Safety Considerations

RFID technology in Sesami is completely safe for children. The low-frequency waves are non-ionizing and have been extensively tested for use in consumer products. The technology meets all FCC and international safety standards for children's toys.
