"By combining AI-powered voice cloning technology with evidence-based developmental activities in a familiar, comforting teddy bear form factor, Sesami can significantly reduce passive screen time while actively supporting language acquisition, cognitive development, and emotional well-being in children ages 18 months to 5 years, providing parents with both peace of mind and actionable insights into their child's growth."

Thesis Components

The Problem

Excessive screen time is harming early childhood development, yet parents need engagement solutions during daily responsibilities.

The Solution

An interactive companion that uses parent voice cloning to create familiar, comforting, developmentally-focused engagement.

The Innovation

AI voice cloning combined with child development science in an emotionally resonant teddy bear form factor.

The Outcome

Reduced screen time, improved developmental outcomes, and empowered parents with data-driven insights.

Screen Time Crisis

Infants recognize parent voices from birth. Studies show familiar voices activate reward centers and reduce cortisol levels in children.

3

Active engagement produces 40% better learning outcomes than passive consumption (Hirsh-Pasek et al., 2015).
