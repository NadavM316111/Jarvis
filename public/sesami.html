Meet Sesami, Your Child's First Friend

An interactive early childhood educational companion designed to support children's development through playful, engaging interaction. Built into a teddy bear with an integrated speaker, Sesami communicates using a cloned version of the parent's voice, creating a familiar and personalized experience.

Explore Sesami Watch Demo

Why Parents Love Sesami

🎤

Parent Voice Cloning

Sesami speaks with your voice, creating a comforting and familiar experience for your child even when you're not there.

🧩

Developmental Activities

Encourages language development, attention, curiosity, motor skills, and early problem-solving through active play.

📊

Progress Insights

Parents receive detailed insights into their child's developmental progress, supporting healthy cognitive and emotional growth.
