Road to Revolution
+
The Stamp Act (1765) imposed direct taxes on colonists, sparking "No Taxation Without Representation"

The First Continental Congress met in 1774 to coordinate colonial resistance
Thomas Paine's "Common Sense" (1776) galvanized public opinion toward independence
"These are the times that try men's souls." - Thomas Paine

+
"The shot heard round the world" at Lexington and Concord (April 1775) began armed conflict
The Declaration of Independence was adopted July 4, 1776, written primarily by Thomas Jefferson
George Washington's crossing of the Delaware and victory at Trenton (1776) saved the revolution
The American victory at Saratoga (1777) convinced France to enter the war as an ally
The brutal winter at Valley Forge (1777-1778) forged a professional Continental Army
British surrender at Yorktown (1781) effectively ended the war; Treaty of Paris signed 1783
"We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal."

Constitution & New Government
+
The Articles of Confederation proved too weak, with no power to tax or regulate commerce
Shays' Rebellion (1786-1787) exposed the need for a stronger central government
The Constitutional Convention (1787) created a new framework balancing federal and state power
The Great Compromise established bicameral Congress; the 3/5 Compromise addressed slavery
The Federalist Papers, written by Hamilton, Madison, and Jay, argued for ratification
The Bill of Rights (first 10 amendments) was added in 1791 to protect individual liberties
George Washington became the first President, establishing precedents still followed today

+
Fort Sumter (April 1861) marked the beginning of America's bloodiest conflict

+
